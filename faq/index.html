Do you work with agencies we already have in place?

Sometimes, though we’re most effective when we own the full strategy for a channel rather than executing someone else’s plan.

Can we start with just one service?

Yes. Most clients start with one or two services and expand once the first channel is proven.

What happens if it’s not working after a few months?

We review the data honestly at each monthly call. If something isn’t working, Fieldstone Growth Agency will tell you and adjust the plan rather than continuing to bill for it.

How is reporting handled?

Every Fieldstone Growth Agency client gets a live dashboard plus a monthly written report and call — no waiting until the end of a quarter to find out what happened.

Do you require a long-term contract?

No. After an initial 60-day onboarding period, engagements run month-to-month with 30 days’ notice to pause or cancel.

Do you guarantee specific results?

No, and we’d be skeptical of any agency that does. We’ll show you realistic ranges based on comparable engagements during the discovery call.

What’s the onboarding process like?

Onboarding covers the audit, access handoff, and a 90-day roadmap review — typically two to three weeks before campaigns go fully live.

Do you offer a discount for annual commitments?

We can discuss annual terms during the proposal stage, though most clients prefer the flexibility of month-to-month billing.
