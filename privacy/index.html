P01Who is responsible for your information

Fieldstone Growth Agency is a trading brand of BONNIE J CHRISTENSEN LLC, a California limited liability company registered at 6900 Seville Ave, Huntington Park, CA 90255-4970 under CA LLC file number 202253412781. BONNIE J CHRISTENSEN LLC is the controller of any personal information collected through this website and is the entity you contract with if an engagement follows.

Questions about this policy, or any request relating to your personal information, should go to agency@bonniejchristensen-marketing.com or +1 (323) 418-2760. Written requests may be sent to the registered address above, marked for the attention of the data protection contact.

P02Information you give us directly

If you use the enquiry form or write to us, we receive the details you choose to provide: your name, company, work email address, telephone number where given, website address, an indicated budget range and whatever you write in the message field.

We ask for a budget range because it determines whether a retainer is viable at all. That answer is used for assessment and is not shared outside BONNIE J CHRISTENSEN LLC.

P03Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider records standard server log data when a page is requested, which may include the requesting IP address, user agent string, referring page and the time of the request. This data supports security, abuse prevention and diagnostics.

Where analytics or conversion-measurement technologies are in use on this site, they are described in our Cookie Policy. Reporting derived from them is reviewed in aggregate; we do not use it to build advertising profiles about individual visitors to this site.

P04Why we process it, and on what basis

We process enquiry information to respond to you, to assess whether we are a suitable firm for the work, and to prepare a proposal if both sides want one. Where an engagement begins, we process information to deliver the services and to meet our accounting and tax obligations.

Depending on where you are located, the lawful basis for this processing is our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries, the steps necessary to enter into a contract with you, your consent where consent is required, or compliance with a legal obligation.

P05Advertising and measurement technology

As a marketing firm we work with advertising and analytics platforms on behalf of clients. That work happens inside client-owned accounts, under the client's own agreements with those platforms; it is separate from this website.

BONNIE J CHRISTENSEN LLC does not sell personal information, and does not share personal information with third parties for their own independent marketing purposes.

P06Service providers

We use a small number of providers to operate this website and our business: static web hosting, email, calendaring, document storage and accounting software. These providers process information only on our instructions and under contractual confidentiality and security obligations.

Some providers may process information outside your country of residence. Where required, transfers are made under an appropriate legal mechanism such as standard contractual clauses.

P07How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are reviewed periodically and deleted when they are no longer relevant to a live business conversation. Records connected to an engagement are kept for the period required by contract, and by applicable tax and accounting law.

You may ask us to delete an enquiry sooner, and we will do so unless we are required to retain it.

P08Your rights

Subject to your jurisdiction, you may have the right to request access to the personal information BONNIE J CHRISTENSEN LLC holds about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, and to receive a copy in a portable format.

California residents may additionally have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to know what categories of personal information are collected and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. BONNIE J CHRISTENSEN LLC does not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under that statute.

To exercise any right, contact agency@bonniejchristensen-marketing.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

P09Children

This website offers business-to-business services and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 16. If you believe a child has provided information through this site, contact agency@bonniejchristensen-marketing.com and we will delete it.

P10Changes to this policy

BONNIE J CHRISTENSEN LLC may update this policy to reflect changes to the website, our providers or applicable law. The version published on this page is the current one, and continued use of the site after a change indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
